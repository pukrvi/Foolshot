# Initial allocation and loss review · 24 November 2026

Intraday describes closing within a session. Both shares and options can trade intraday. For the first paper cohort, use whole cash shares with a ₹10,000 maximum position, one open position, ₹0 in options and ₹90,000 idle at inception. No minimum spend or required trades. Options require contract lots and introduce expiry, time value and a different cost structure. Test a separate hypothetical options book later on the same dates with quoted spreads and all costs.

Dhan prices intraday equity brokerage at the lower of ₹20 or 0.03% per executed order, while options brokerage is ₹20 per executed order. At ₹10,000 on each side of one equity round trip, illustrative brokerage alone is ₹6; options entry and exit cost ₹40 brokerage before other charges. Lower capital changes the fee burden. [Dhan pricing](https://dhan.co/pricing/)

SEBI studied individual equity-cash intraday outcomes and equity derivatives outcomes in distinct populations and periods. Both motivate measuring net results and keeping initial exposure small. Neither identifies a profitable allocation or an optimal streak length. [Cash intraday study](https://www.sebi.gov.in/reports-and-statistics/research/jul-2024/study-analysis-of-intraday-trading-by-individuals-in-equity-cash-segment_84946.html) · [Derivatives study](https://www.sebi.gov.in/sebi_data/attachdocs/aug-2026/1787236407005.pdf)

A proposed paper baseline is two distinct entry attempts per session and a review on three consecutive closed net losses across sessions. Passes, unfilled attempts and duplicate receipts do not count as losses. Brokerage is generally tied to execution. An attempt cap also bounds repeated submissions and model loops. Three losses are a diagnostic trigger. Even three independent 50/50 trades have a 12.5% chance of all losing in a specified block. Inspect software, costs, evidence and fills before changing strategy. Compare one versus two attempts and ₹5,000 versus ₹10,000 on matched dates, keeping every no-trade day. Use a later untouched interval before promoting a change.

Daily limit candidate ₹350, including realised and open trading losses and estimated charges. Two ₹125 planned losses total ₹250; the extra ₹100 allows for gaps and estimation error. Actual losses can exceed any trigger. Measure from the opening trading balance; record peak drawdown for analysis only. Daily close reserves only excess over the ₹1 lakh principal. A manual transfer of that reserve has zero P&L effect.
