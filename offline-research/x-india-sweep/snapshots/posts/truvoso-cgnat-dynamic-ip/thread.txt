This is the core technical truth that prop firms choose to ignore.

In major trading hubs like India, Pakistan, Bangladesh, and Nigeria, Internet Service Providers (ISPs) operate on massive dynamic networks and Carrier-Grade NAT (CGNAT) systems.

In India: Telecom giants like Reliance Jio dynamically rotate public IP addresses constantly. Your IP can change every single time your phone switches towers or reconnects to data,even after this if you want Static IP then Static IPS cost 40k-1 lakh.

In Nigeria: Major networks like MTN and Airtel assign shared public IPs to thousands of users simultaneously.

What does this mean in plain English? Two completely unrelated traders, sitting in the same neighborhood, using the same ISP network, can easily be assigned the exact same public IP address by the network tower that day.
