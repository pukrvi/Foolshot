Pranjal Soni (@pranjalsoni_): "how to get lucky in trading, every day, with claude

3 days of real trades. 3 green days

people think i found some secret. i didn't. i just stopped doing the dumb stuff

every idea gets tested on 2 years of data before it gets my money. most of them die there

some days there's nothing good. we don't trade. that's fine

nothing stays open overnight. i sleep

day 2 my wifi changed ip mid trade and the bot couldn't exit. i just sat there watching. moved everything to a server that night. never again
