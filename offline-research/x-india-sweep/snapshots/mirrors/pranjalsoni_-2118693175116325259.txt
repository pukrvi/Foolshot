Pranjal Soni (@pranjalsoni_): "day 5 of letting an ai trade my real money. second red day in a row

woke up on time today. logged in. felt ready

the bot didn't. a bug i shipped last night made it think the market was closed for a holiday. paper trading switched itself off at 9:16 on a normal friday

fixed it, restarted, moved on

around 10 we found a setup that actually held up in 2 years of data. stocks that gap hard on news and keep falling

claude said paper it first. i said go full size

> stopped out in 13 minutes. -3.7k

the stock bounced right back down after. of course

then i got desperate and wanted to scalp. 1-3 minute trades, grab cash, get out

so we tested it. 20k trades on 1-minute data: zero edge before costs, guaranteed loss after

ran it live on paper anyway with 5 lakh a trade

> 72 trades in 4 hours. -30k. on paper. thank god

two days, two lessons:
- don't trade what the rules say to skip
- don't bet big on something you found an hour ago

account is down ~1.6% over 5 days. not dead. just humbled

weekend plan: test the one real setup on 5 more years of data, build a model, and hard-code the risk rules so i can't override them

see you monday" | teapawt teapawt
