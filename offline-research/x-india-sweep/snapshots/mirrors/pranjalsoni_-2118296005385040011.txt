Pranjal Soni (@pranjalsoni_): "day 4 of letting an ai trade my real money. 

first red day

woke up late. logged in at 1pm. that one mistake decided the whole day

the bot had one good setup this morning. a stock dumping hard on big volume, short it early. it was ready at 9:45 and i was asleep. replayed it after close: would've been +11k on paper size

by 1pm the market had no direction. half up, half down. our rule says don't trade days like that

claude even said skip. then i said use the margin, and it found me trades anyway

shorted a stock that had already fallen 6%. the market kept ripping up. stock bounced, stop hit. done

> -3.6k on the day. every loss stayed inside the stop we set before entering

account is still green across 4 days. just way less green

what changed tonight:
the bot now starts on its own at 8:50, login or not
my phone gets pinged if it's not running
no trade on a no-trade day. even if i beg

3 green days, 1 red. the red one was 100% me

day 5 tomorrow. waking up early" | teapawt teapawt
