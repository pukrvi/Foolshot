Pranjal Soni (@pranjalsoni_): "day 3 of letting claude trade my real money.

day 1 went too well. first real trades, small size, closed green. i felt like a genius

day 2 humbled me. my home internet changed ip in the middle of a trade. the broker only accepts orders from one ip, so the bot couldn't exit. i watched the position sit there until the broker closed it for me. still green, but that was luck, not skill

then i lost two market days to my own setup issues. no trades. annoying

so today i fixed the root problem. rented a small server in mumbai, gave it a fixed ip, moved everything there. now i log in with one tap from my phone and my wifi can die, doesn't matter

first order from the new server was 1 share. just to see if the pipes work. they did

the market was flat all day but money was rotating hard underneath. the bot found a few names, i said yes to each one. three worked. one didn't, and that one was my idea, not the bot's. cut it early

> everything closed on its own before 3:15. green after charges

the best part came after close. i was sure we'd have made more by entering earlier. so we tested it on 2 years of data

i was wrong. the obvious version loses money. one small piece held up, so that goes to paper first

3 trading days, 3 green. same rules every day: my yes on every real trade, stops always on, nothing held overnight

day 4 tomorrow" | teapawt teapawt
